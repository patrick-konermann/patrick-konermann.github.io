excerpt: 'Directed cash flow linkages give rise to cross-predictability of returns between linked assets solely through equilibrium pricing effects in a frictionless, informationally efficient, and complete market. The risk-based mechanism builds on fundamental principles of economically linked assets. In a consumption-based equilibrium asset pricing model which allows for closed-form solutions, we dissect expected excess return differentials into three channels. Cross-predictability exists under two key conditions. First, cash flow linkages must have a direction, but cross-predictability arises irrespective of the direction itself. Second, there must be cross-sectional differences in the strengths of the linkages.'
