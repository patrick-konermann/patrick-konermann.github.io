excerpt: 'Directed cash flow linkages give rise to cross-predictability of returns between linked assets solely through equilibrium pricing effects in a frictionless, informationally efficient, and complete market. The risk-based mechanism builds on fundamental principles of economically linked assets. In a consumption-based equilibrium asset pricing model which allows for closed-form solutions, expected excess returns are tied to differences in the strengths of cash flow linkages. Our equilibrium mechanism generates cross-predictability under two conditions. First, cash flow linkages must have a direction. Second, there must be cross-sectional heterogeneity in the strengths of these linkages.'
