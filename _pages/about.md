I am an Associate Professor (with tenure) at the Department of Finance at BI Norwegian Business School in Oslo, Norway. I joined BI in 2016, after a postdoctoral position at the Finance Center of the University of Muenster, where I also received my Dr. rer. pol. in Finance in 2014 (supervisor: Nicole Branger).

My research is in asset pricing. I am particularly interested in financial networks, heterogeneous agent models, and market incompleteness. My work has been published in the *Review of Finance*, the *Journal of Financial and Quantitative Analysis*, and the *Review of Financial Economics*.

From 2022 to 2023, and again in the second half of 2025, I served as Associate Dean for the [MSc in Finance](https://www.bi.edu/) at BI.

**Research interests:** Asset Pricing, Financial Networks, Heterogeneous Agent Models, Market Incompleteness
